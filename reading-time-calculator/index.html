How the estimate works

We assume about 275 words per page, the usual figure for a printed novel, and divide by your reading speed. Small-print paperbacks and dense fantasy run higher; large-print and dialogue-heavy thrillers run lower, so treat it as a close guess, not a stopwatch.

Questions

How long does it take to read 300 pages?

At 250 words per minute it takes about 5 hours and 30 minutes, which is around 11 days at 30 minutes a day.

Is listening faster than reading?

For most people, no. Audiobooks are narrated at about 150 words per minute, so a book usually takes longer to listen to than to read. The upside is that you can listen while you cook, commute or walk.
